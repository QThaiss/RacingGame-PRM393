AUDIO FILES REQUIRED


Place the following MP3 files in this folder to enable sound:

  bg_music.mp3   - Background music (looped on Home screen)
  race_start.mp3 - Plays when lights-out countdown begins
  race_bg.mp3    - Looped during the race
  win.mp3        - Plays when player wins (bet on winner)
  lose.mp3       - Plays when player loses (bet on loser)

Free sources:
  https://freesound.org
  https://pixabay.com/music/

The app runs fine without these files (sound is silently skipped).
